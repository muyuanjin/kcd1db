First release for Kingdom Come: Deliverance on Windows x64 Steam build 1.9.8-404-504czj4 (BuildID 22623230). Lua mods can save progress with individual saves and share settings across playthroughs. Close the game and extract kcd1db.asi and the bundled x64 dinput8.dll into KingdomComeDeliverance/Bin/Win64, beside KingdomCome.exe. Keep your existing dinput8.dll if you already use an ASI loader. Back up kcd1db.db alongside your saves to retain mod data.
